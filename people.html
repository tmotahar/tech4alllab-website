01 About the lab

Research shaped by lived experience.

We study how technology can expand independence, participation, health, and well-being for people with disabilities. Our work brings together computing, design, rehabilitation, and community knowledge.

We partner with disabled people, caregivers, clinicians, educators, and community organizations throughout the research process—from identifying meaningful problems to evaluating technologies in the places where life happens.
